# Monarch United Fantasy — league rules

Transcribed from the Yahoo league settings (league 894700) on 2026-10-14. Source of truth is Yahoo; this file exists so `search_league_history` can answer rules questions in the league's own terms. One `##` section = one retrieval chunk (MUFF-63).

## League format
Head-to-head, up to 16 teams (14 active in 2026), no divisions, no median or second-opponent games. Scoring starts week 1. Live standard draft, 90 seconds per pick, held Tuesday September 8 at 7pm MDT. Not a cash league. Whole points only: fractional points are off, negative points are on. Benched players are not locked. Eliminated teams are locked.

## Roster
Starting lineup: QB, WR, WR, RB, RB, TE, one W/R/T flex, K, DEF. Six bench spots (BN), no IR slot listed. Fifteen roster spots total. Can't-cut list from Yahoo Sports.

## Scoring — offense
Standard scoring, not PPR: receptions are worth nothing. Passing: 1 point per 50 passing yards, 6 per passing touchdown, -2 per interception. Rushing: 1 point per 20 rushing yards, 6 per rushing touchdown. Receiving: 1 point per 20 receiving yards, 6 per receiving touchdown. Returns: 1 point per 50 return yards, 6 per return touchdown. 2 per 2-point conversion, -2 per fumble lost, 6 for an offensive fumble return touchdown. Passing yards and touchdowns are set above Yahoo defaults (50 yards per point vs 25, 6 per TD vs 4); rushing and receiving yards are below default (20 per point vs 10).

## Scoring — kickers
Field goals: 3 points from 0-19, 20-29 and 30-39 yards; 4 points from 40-49; 5 points from 50 or more. 1 point per extra point made.

## Scoring — defense and special teams
1 per sack, 2 per interception, 2 per fumble recovery, 6 per touchdown, 2 per safety, 2 per blocked kick, 2 per extra point returned. Points allowed: 0 points allowed scores 10; 1-6 scores 7; 7-13 scores 4; 14-20 scores 1; 21-27 scores 0; 28-34 scores -1; 35 or more scores -4.

## Waivers and free agents
No waivers: free agents are first come, first served (waiver time is none, weekly waivers none, continual rolling list on paper only). No maximum acquisitions per week or per season. Post-draft players follow the same rules.

## Trades
No limit on trades. Trade deadline November 28, 2026. Trades are reviewed by league vote with Yahoo's default veto threshold; a trade is rejected or accepted after 1 day. Draft pick trades are not allowed.

## Playoffs
8 of the 14 teams make the playoffs, played over weeks 15, 16 and 17, ending Monday January 4. No reseeding between rounds. Tie-breaker for playoff seeding: best regular-season record head-to-head against the opponent.
